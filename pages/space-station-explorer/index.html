Space Station Explorer Online

A text-based exploration game set on a space station, played in the browser. A Flask web app with accounts, where each account owns one character whose progress is saved on the server.

Core features

  • Accounts

    Register with a username, a password and a security question; each account owns exactly one character, saved after every action. Forgot password? resets with the answer; five wrong answers lock resets for 15 minutes. A reset logs the account out of every other browser. A security question can't be changed once set, and five wrong passwords in a row lock a username's logins for a minute.

  • Jobs

    Create a character with a job from Staff Assistant to Captain, each with its own access and starting credits.

  • The station

    A hollow-square ring of hallways with Quarters at the junction and Bridge, MedBay, Security, Engineering Bay, Bar and Botany Lab branching off, with random events on the way.

  • Inventory

    Carry items, store them in your quarters locker, hold them in your hands, and examine, read, use, eat, drink or drop them.

  • Health

    Limb damage plus burns, poison and oxygen, treated in MedBay.

  • Crew

    Start alongside procedurally staffed NPC department heads and assistants. Deleting your character erases its progress so you can roll a new one.

  • Player Guide

    A wiki-style guide at /wiki/, readable without logging in, with pages for the basics, each system and each job. Its prices and numbers are read from the game.

Station systems

Power
Battery levels, power allocation to life support, lighting, security and communications, solar charging, and emergency modes. The Engineering panel is for Engineers, the Captain and the Head of Personnel. System faults knock out lighting, security or communications until they are repaired from that panel with Basic Tools or a Repair Tool.
Life support
Oxygen follows power allocation. Switching life support off sends a station-wide emergency announcement, with a short grace period before oxygen damage starts. Underpowered life support damages the player and crew, with an emergency revive if it reaches critical levels.
Medical
Paid treatment and assessments in MedBay. Doctors, the Captain and the Head of Personnel get free full treatment and a crew vitals monitor, where injured crew can be treated and pay from their own credits.
Security
Access control for restricted areas, door locking and unlocking for authorized staff, and a crew manifest for Security. Guards arrest crew with warrants; wanted crew sometimes offer a bribe, unpaid fines are settled with a guard, and the Security station can pardon prisoners. A petty crime a guard witnesses puts out a warrant.
Stock market
A live simulation traded from your quarters computer, with a price history chart, a holdings view of what your shares are worth, filters and a transaction history.
Bar
Order from the menu, mix drinks from the recipe list as the Bartender, and watch the alcohol tally on your character sheet. Drinks are free for the Bartender, and crew drink orders pay the Bartender.
Botany
A vending machine at the botanist station sells seeds, liquids and tools, restocking one item every 5 minutes. Plant across several planters and water before each growth stage or the plant dies. Miracle Grow, Plentiful Harvest, Aloe for burns, harvesting at the station, and a Trowel or Chainsaw to clear dead plants. The Bartender pays the Botanist 50-150 credits for requested produce.
Bridge and personnel
Talk to the ship's leadership, a crew manifest and NPC job assignment for the Head of Personnel, a public terminal for job-change requests, and Bridge door control for the Captain and HoP. Command requests the Captain approves or denies: powering life support, a sector lockdown, Power Saving mode, or a warrant.
Engineering
A fabricator for tools and books, and the full power panel for authorized staff.
Jobs and errands
Every job has its own hallway events. One errand at a time, shown in the station status: Staff Assistants help or bring items to crew for 25-75 credits, the Botanist delivers produce, the Bartender serves drink orders, and the Doctor treats injured crew.

Tips
  • Monitor your health and seek treatment in MedBay when needed.
  • Keep track of power levels and oxygen status from Engineering.
  • Use the stock market on your quarters computer to earn additional credits.
  • Explore all rooms to discover features, items and job-specific stations.
  • Progress saves automatically; Save and Exit returns you to your account page.

Run it

Requires Python 3.10 or newer. Flask's built-in server is fine on your own machine or a LAN.

python -m venv .venv
.venv\Scripts\activate          # Windows; use `source .venv/bin/activate` elsewhere
pip install -r requirements.txt
python run_server.py

Open http://127.0.0.1:5000, register an account, and create your character. Accounts and characters live in instance/space_online.sqlite3: back that folder up to keep your players, delete it to start fresh.

Settings
HOST
Interface to bind, default 127.0.0.1. Use 0.0.0.0 so other machines on your network can connect.
PORT
Port to listen on, default 5000.
SPACE_ONLINE_SECRET_KEY
Session signing key. If unset, one is generated once into instance/secret_key.
SECURE_COOKIES
1 marks the login cookie Secure, for a site served over HTTPS. Off by default.
With Docker 
docker build -t space-station-explorer-online .
docker run -d -p 5000:5000 -v space-online-data:/app/instance space-station-explorer-online

The Dockerfile runs the game under gunicorn on port 5000, with the data in a volume at /app/instance. Set SPACE_ONLINE_SECRET_KEY with -e so logins survive the key file being lost.

On every start the container runs docker/git-sync.sh, which pulls the latest commit on main and reinstalls requirements.txt when that commit is new, so a pushed commit goes live on the next restart. If GitHub can't be reached it starts on the code it has. REPO_URL empty runs exactly what is in the image; REPO_BRANCH, SYNC_DIR and BUILD_CMD change the branch, folder and install step.

Log in with a portfolio account

With PORTFOLIO_SSO_URL and PORTFOLIO_SSO_PUBLIC_KEY set, the login page offers to log in with a portfolio account, and reads Log In as plus the portfolio username when this browser is logged in there. The first such login links an account you already have, or makes a new one named after the portfolio username, with a number added if that name is taken. Every later portfolio login from any device opens the linked account.

A new account has no password of its own, so the portfolio is the only way into it. Without those two settings the app runs on its own.

Tests and project layout 
pip install -r requirements-dev.txt
python -m pytest
npm install
npm test

The tests cover accounts, password resets, portfolio login, character creation, playing and saving, and the game itself: every room and station, items and hands, crew movement, jail, power, oxygen, the stock market, botany and the hallway events. Coverage commands fail below 100%; the Docker image serves both reports at /coverage/.
